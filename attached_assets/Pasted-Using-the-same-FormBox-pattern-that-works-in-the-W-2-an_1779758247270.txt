PATH B (Freelance / Schedule C):

  1099-NEC anatomy
    Banner: "1099-NEC · Nonemployee Compensation"
    Boxes to include:
      Box 1: Nonemployee compensation ($X amount)
      Box 4: Federal income tax withheld (usually $0)
      Box 5-7: State info (state name, state tax 
              withheld, payer's state number)
    Tooltips should explain: who sends it (clients), 
    when it arrives (by January 31), and where Box 1 
    lands on the return (Schedule C, Part I, Line 1).

  1099-K anatomy
    Banner: "1099-K · Payment Card and Third Party 
    Network Transactions"
    Boxes to include:
      Box 1a: Gross amount of payment card/third party
              network transactions
      Box 4: Federal income tax withheld (usually $0)
      Box 5a-l: Monthly amounts (just show 2-3 months 
                as representative)
    Tooltip on Box 1a: explains this is gross receipts 
    before any platform fees or refunds, and that the 
    taxable amount on Schedule C will usually be less 
    than this number after expenses.

  Schedule C anatomy (simplified — not the full form)
    Banner: "Schedule C · Profit or Loss From Business"
    Structure to show (in stacked sections rather than 
    boxes):
      Part I — Income
        Line 1: Gross receipts or sales
        Line 7: Gross income
      Part II — Expenses (show 4-5 representative 
      categories)
        Line 8: Advertising
        Line 18: Office expense
        Line 22: Supplies
        Line 25: Utilities
        Line 27a: Other expenses
      Part II — Bottom
        Line 28: Total expenses
        Line 31: Net profit or (loss)
    Tooltip on Line 31: explains this is the number 
    that flows to Schedule 1, then to Form 1040, and 
    becomes the base for both income tax and 
    self-employment tax.

  Schedule SE anatomy (simplified)
    Banner: "Schedule SE · Self-Employment Tax"
    Structure to show:
      Net SE earnings (from Schedule C Line 31)
      × 92.35% adjustment
      = SE income subject to tax
      × 15.3% (= 12.4% SS + 2.9% Medicare)
      = Total SE tax
      ÷ 2 = deductible half
    Tooltip on the 15.3%: explains this is what a 
    W-2 employee and their employer pay TOGETHER, 
    but as self-employed you pay both halves yourself.
    Tooltip on deductible half: explains this gets 
    deducted on Schedule 1, partially offsetting the 
    income tax burden of SE tax.

PATH D (Investment / Interest / Retirement):

  1099-INT anatomy
    Banner: "1099-INT · Interest Income"
    Boxes:
      Box 1: Interest income
      Box 2: Early withdrawal penalty
      Box 3: Interest on U.S. Savings Bonds and 
             Treasury obligations
      Box 4: Federal income tax withheld
    Tooltip on Box 1: lands on Schedule B (or directly 
    on Form 1040 if interest under $1,500 total).

  1099-DIV anatomy
    Banner: "1099-DIV · Dividends and Distributions"
    Boxes:
      Box 1a: Total ordinary dividends
      Box 1b: Qualified dividends
      Box 2a: Total capital gain distributions
      Box 4: Federal income tax withheld
    Tooltip on Box 1b: explains qualified dividends 
    are taxed at lower long-term capital gains rates, 
    not ordinary income rates — this is the most 
    misunderstood box on this form.

  1099-B anatomy
    Banner: "1099-B · Proceeds from Broker Transactions"
    Boxes:
      Box 1a: Description of property
      Box 1c: Date sold
      Box 1d: Proceeds
      Box 1e: Cost or other basis
      Box 2: Type of gain or loss (short-term vs 
             long-term)
    Tooltip on Box 1d vs Box 1e: explains that the 
    gain (or loss) is Proceeds minus Basis — and that 
    if Basis is blank, taxpayer must determine it 
    themselves.

  1099-R anatomy
    Banner: "1099-R · Distributions from Pensions, 
    Annuities, Retirement Plans"
    Boxes:
      Box 1: Gross distribution
      Box 2a: Taxable amount
      Box 4: Federal income tax withheld
      Box 7: Distribution code(s)
    Tooltip on Box 7: explains this code determines 
    whether the distribution is subject to early-
    withdrawal penalty, is a rollover, is normal 
    retirement, etc. The most important box on the 
    form even though it's just a letter.

For all anatomies: match the visual pattern from the 
existing W-2 anatomy on Path A. Hover or focus reveals 
a tooltip BELOW the form (not floating), with the box 
label, description, and "where it lands" section.
